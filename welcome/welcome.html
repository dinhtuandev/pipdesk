Three clicks to float

  1. 1

    Pin the sites you float

    Toolbar icon → Float button on this site. A small badge then sits over that page's video.

  2. 2

    Pick what floats

    AltShiftP tab · AltShiftR region · AltShiftX stop. Video mode lives in the popup: the page's own PiP needs your click.

  3. 3

    Arrange, then keep working

    Each floating source gets a row: Show alone, Back to grid, Stop. Your notes, to-dos and timer can join the same window.
