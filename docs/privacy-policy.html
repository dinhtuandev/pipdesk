PiPDesk privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-28

PiPDesk is a Chrome extension that shows content you choose in a floating picture-in-picture window. This policy explains what the extension handles and what it never does.

What PiPDesk handles

  • Captured pixels. When you float a tab or a region, Chrome's tabCapture API hands the extension a live video stream of that tab. The stream is decoded and drawn onto a canvas inside a hidden offscreen document in your browser. It is never recorded to disk, never sent over a network, and is released when you stop the capture or close the floating window.
  • Page interaction. When you start the region selector or the video picture-in-picture action, the extension injects a small script into the tab you are on. It draws a selection overlay, or asks the page's video element to enter picture-in-picture. It reads no page content beyond what that action needs.
  • Your own data. Notes, to-dos, focus-timer history and settings are stored with chrome.storage.local on your device. If you turn on "Sync across devices", the notes, to-dos and timer data are mirrored to chrome.storage.sync, which means it is stored in your own Chrome profile by Google under Google's terms.
  • The floating button setting. If you enable the floating badge for a site, the extension stores that site's origin and asks Chrome for permission to run on that site so the badge can appear there.

What PiPDesk never does

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or advertising.
  • No network requests of any kind. The extension contains no remote code and loads nothing from the internet.
  • No selling, sharing or transferring of user data to third parties.
  • No reading of browsing history, passwords, form fields or page text for any purpose other than the action you started.

Retention and deletion

Captured video exists only in memory while a capture is running. Notes, to-dos, timer history and settings stay in chrome.storage until you clear them in the extension or uninstall PiPDesk, which removes its storage. If sync is enabled, delete the data in the extension and it is removed from chrome.storage.sync as well; you can also clear synced extension data from your Google account's Chrome sync settings.

Permissions

Each permission exists for one visible feature: activeTab and tabCapture to float the tab you chose, offscreen to run the compositor, scripting for the region selector and video action, storage for your notes and settings, alarms and notifications for the focus timer and auto-close, contextMenus for Chrome's right-click menu, and optional host access only for sites where you explicitly enable the floating badge. The YouTube content script places the Quick PiP control in the player bar.

Children

PiPDesk is a general-purpose productivity tool and is not directed at children.
